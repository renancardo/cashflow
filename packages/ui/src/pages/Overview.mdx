# Pages

Templates filled with realistic or fixture-driven data. Pages are the canonical Storybook preview for engine-driven states before full app wiring ([US-1.9](../../../../docs/user-stories/01-projection-engine.md)).

**Current pages:** `AccountsScreen` (fixture-driven accounts list with loading, empty, and error states)

Examples to add:

- Year calendar with `basic-salary-rent` fixture projection
- Month calendar with below-buffer days highlighted
- Empty onboarding state

See [ADR-007](../../../../docs/decisions/ADR-007-storybook-vitest.md) for the full taxonomy.
